PROTOTYPE POLICY

Privacy notice

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Blue Horizon AI Studio is an early front-end prototype. This notice describes the behavior of the current static website and browser-based workspace. It is not a substitute for a final privacy policy reviewed for the commercial product.

Documents and image text

The Studio stores documents, folder names, and editor content in the local storage of the browser on the device where you use it. This prototype does not sync those records to a Blue Horizon AI server or user account. If you insert an image into a document, its data can be stored inside the local document record and may consume browser storage.

Use Export to download copies of important work. Avoid using this prototype on a shared device for private documents. Anyone who can access the same browser profile may be able to access the locally stored workspace. Clearing browser storage may remove locally saved documents.

Uploaded images

When you select an image in Image Text Studio, the browser loads it locally to create the canvas preview and PNG. The prototype does not upload that image to a Blue Horizon AI server. Only use images you have permission to use.

Contact form

If the owner has configured the optional Web3Forms contact integration, the name, reply email, topic, and message you submit are sent to Web3Forms and forwarded to the site owner. Web3Forms processes submissions under its own privacy policy and service terms. If that integration is not configured, the form attempts to prepare a message in your device's email application; the webpage itself does not send that email. Do not submit confidential or sensitive information through contact forms.

Contact processor

When enabled by the owner, the contact form uses Web3Forms (https://web3forms.com) to deliver form submissions. The access key used by this form is intended for public website use; it is not a payment secret or AI API key. See Web3Forms privacy information and verify the current provider terms before launch.

AI and analytics

The current Studio does not call an AI API, run subscription billing, or intentionally send document text to an AI provider. It does not include an account system or an analytics SDK. Google Fonts may be requested by your browser when you are online, and the hosting provider may process ordinary request logs after the site is published.

Future changes

Before adding accounts, cloud backups, AI generation, subscription payments, analytics, or integrations, Blue Horizon AI Studio will need an updated notice explaining what data is processed, by whom, for what purpose, and how users can manage or delete it. Do not enter sensitive, regulated, or confidential data in this prototype.
