GOOD TO KNOW

Clear about what
works today.

Does the Studio use real AI right now?

No. The current editor, local document manager, and image-text generator run in the browser. AI-connected features are planned but not active.

Where are my documents saved?

Documents are stored in this browser's local storage on this device. They do not sync to an account or cloud server. Export important work regularly and do not use a shared computer for private documents.

Can I make the K or number itself out of an image?

Yes. In Image Text Studio, type the letter or number, choose a texture or upload your own image, then export the result as a transparent PNG. The image becomes the fill inside the character shapes.

Can I export to Word or PDF?

The current prototype exports HTML, plain text, JSON backups, and transparent PNG artwork. For a PDF, use the browser's print dialog and select Save as PDF. Direct DOCX export is not included yet.

Are the five subscriptions available?

No. The prices are proposed test ranges. There is no checkout and no AI credit allowance has been activated. We will not charge anyone until the paid product is implemented and the terms are clear.
