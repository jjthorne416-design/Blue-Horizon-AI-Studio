# Blue Horizon AI Studio — customer research templates

Use these messages honestly. Do not say the Studio is a live AI product, do not promise a launch date, and do not pressure anyone to buy. The current public prototype has local document storage, editable header designs, image-text exports, and email-draft feedback; it does not have accounts, cloud sync, live AI, or billing.

## Short introduction

Hi! I'm testing a small browser-based workspace that combines document writing, reusable page headers, and image-filled typography. I'm speaking with people who create documents or graphics to understand where their current process is slow or frustrating. Would you be open to a 10-minute chat and a quick prototype walkthrough? No purchase or signup is required.

## Interview questions

1. What is the last document or text graphic you created?
2. Which applications did you use and why?
3. Did you have to move between a writing tool and a design tool?
4. How did you apply a header or branded style?
5. Have you ever wanted the inside of letters or numbers to be filled with a photo or texture?
6. How often do you repeat that workflow?
7. What file formats do you need to import or export?
8. What is the most frustrating step?
9. Would you use a combined editor and image-text tool? What is missing for it to be genuinely useful?
10. Would solving this problem be worth paying for? What price would seem fair, and why?

## Follow-up after a walkthrough

Thanks for taking a look at the Blue Horizon AI Studio prototype. I'm particularly interested in what felt confusing, what you'd use repeatedly, and what would need to work before you could rely on it for a real project. Could you share the one or two improvements that would matter most?

## Simple tracking sheet fields

- Date and customer segment
- Customer's most recent relevant task
- Current tools and workaround
- How often task occurs
- Impact (time, mistakes, friction, cost)
- Features requested without prompting
- Result of prototype walkthrough
- Repeat-use intent and evidence
- Price reaction and reason
- Next action / follow-up date

Do not collect sensitive customer records or confidential business documents during early research. Record only what is needed to understand the workflow.
